01 / CHECK YOUR FIT

Match the role to your evidence.

AI compares a posting with your career knowledge, shows which requirements you can support, flags gaps, and suggests questions to ask the recruiter.

02 / TAILOR YOUR APPLICATION

Stop writing from a blank page.

Get help tailoring resumes, drafting cover letters, improving weak bullets, and answering application questions. Review proposed edits before accepting them.

03 / PREPARE & PRACTISE

Go in with your stories ready.

Build an interview preparation plan, rehearse typed or spoken answers, and get feedback. Use your own experience to sharpen the story you want to tell.

04 / YOUR CAREER COACH

An AI with your work in context.

Talk through your next move, turn accomplishments into interview stories, and draft recruiter replies. Bring selected facts, documents, and applications into the conversation.

Your AI advantage.

Your career knowledge gives the AI something specific to work with: what you did, what changed, and what matters in your next role. Curate it once and use it across fit checks, applications, coaching, and interview preparation.

Connect a supported AI provider or compatible endpoint, including local models. Control which facts and stories reach the AI, how much context it receives, and its reasoning effort and time limits.
