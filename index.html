An AI-powered career and job-search workspace. Built for individual job seekers.

Your experience, connected

A job search has a lot of moving parts.
Keep the context between them.

01 / KNOWLEDGE

Start with what you’ve done.

Collect accomplishments, skills, preferences, and interview stories. Build a career record you can return to for the next opportunity.

02 / DOCUMENTS

Make the experience relevant.

Build resumes and cover letters, tailor them to a role, and review proposed AI edits before accepting them. Export PDF, Word, Markdown, or JSON.

03 / APPLICATIONS

Remember what went where.

Keep postings, contacts, stages, notes, follow-ups, and the documents you sent together in each application’s workspace.

04 / INTERVIEWS

Have your stories ready.

Prepare answers and practise interviews using your own experience. Keep debriefs and recruiter messages with the opportunity.

Documentation screenshots use fictional demo data and show the shared Reactive Resume foundation with upstream branding. All That Work’s current interface uses our blue asterisk logo.

You choose the model and the context

Your search.
Your AI connection.

Connect a supported AI provider or compatible endpoint, including local models. Decide what career information it receives and review suggestions before using them.

All That Work adds knowledge curation, relevance ranking, per-account context and timing limits, and model reasoning controls to its Reactive Resume foundation.

Alex Iordanescu Founder & maintainer

I use All That Work in my own job search. I maintain this independent fork to keep career knowledge useful across documents, applications, and interviews, with more control over AI context and limits.

The self-hosted application is actively developed and used today. I’m developing a paid hosted service for people who want the workspace without running their own infrastructure. Public signups aren’t open yet, and pricing is still being developed.
